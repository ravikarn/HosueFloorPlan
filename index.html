Executive Summary & Bihar Building Byelaws 2026 Amendment Overview

7 Statutory Amendments Applied

Following the enactment of the Bihar Building Byelaws 2026 (approved by the Bihar Cabinet in November 2026, repealing the Bihar Building Bye-Laws 2014 under Chapter VIII, Byelaw 41), the original 30'0" × 60'0" design for the 33'0" × 66'0" (202.34 m²) North-facing plot on a 33'0" (10.06 m) road was audited clause-by-clause. While habitable room areas, ceiling height (`3.05 m`), plinth height (`0.60 m`), and rainwater harvesting already complied, **7 statutory deviations** were identified and systematically amended in this updated package.

1. Setbacks & Footprint (Table 13) 29'8" × 54'5"

For a `>9m–12m` (`33'0"`) road, **Table 13 (Row 4)** mandates Front `2.0m (6'7")`, Rear `1.5m (5'0")`, and Side `1.0m (3'4")` setbacks for `10m–15m` buildings (or Front `1.5m`, Rear `0.75m`, Side `NIL` up to `10m`). Providing a full-width **`5'0"` South Rear Setback** gives the South apartment direct rear windows and legal utility balconies.

2. Staircase & Stilt Rules (Table 8B) 1.25m Stair · 2.5m Stilt

Under **Byelaw 23(5)(ii) Table 8(B)**, multi-family flat staircases require **`1.25m (4'1")` flight width**, **`300mm (12")` treads**, and **`≤150mm (6")` risers** (expanded core: `15'0" × 8'2"`). Under **Byelaw 2(136) & 32(3)**, dedicating the Ground Floor to a **`2.50m (8'2.5")` high 6-Car Stilt (`6.6 ECS`)** exempts the entire stilt from FAR and height!

3. Net FAR 2.18 & Fast-Track Permit Base FAR 2.75 / 3.00

Because **Annexure 1 (Item 13a)** counts the staircase only once on the ground floor and exempts the stilt and mumty, our **Net Countable FAR is `2.18`** (Stilt+3) or **`1.48`** (Stilt+2)—requiring **zero Premium FAR fee** under **Table 17 (`2.75`)** or **Table 18 (`3.00`)**.

SEC 04

Technical Conflict, Bihar Building Byelaws 2026 Deviation & Resolution Register

All 2026 Deviations Resolved
CONF-01 · SETBACKS & ZERO REAR LINE VS. BBL 2026 TABLE 13 AMENDED IN 2026 PLAN

Problem: The legacy `30'×60'` footprint had `6'0" (1.83m)` North, `0'0"` South, and `3'0" (0.91m)` East setbacks, violating BBL 2026 Table 13 Row 4 (`>9m–12m` road) (`2.0m` front, `1.5m` rear, `1.0m` side for `10m–15m` height).

Impact: Non-compliant setbacks would block online AutoMap / Single-Window approval and prevent legal rear windows.

Implemented Amendment: Adjusted footprint to **`29'8" × 54'5"` (`1,614 sq.ft.`)**, providing **`6'7" (2.00m)` North**, **`5'0" (1.52m)` full-width South**, and **`3'4" (1.02m)` East** setbacks while retaining `0'0"` on West.

CONF-02 · STAIRCASE WIDTH (0.99m VS. 1.25m) & TREAD (254mm VS. 300mm) AMENDED IN 2026 PLAN

Problem: Legacy `3'3" (0.99m)` flights and `10" (254mm)` treads are only permitted for 1–2 family houses under **Byelaw 23(5)(ii) Table 8(A)**. Multi-family flats fall under **Table 8(B)** (`1.25m` width, `300mm` tread, `≤150mm` riser).

Impact: A `3'3"` stair in an apartment building violates life-safety egress capacity (Table 7 & Table 8B).

Implemented Amendment: Widened the central dog-legged staircase enclosure from `14'6" × 6'6"` to **`15'0" × 8'2"` (`122.5 sq.ft.`)** with **`4'1" (1.25m)` flights**, **`12" (300mm)` treads**, and **`6" (150mm)` risers** (`11 risers/flight ≤ 12 max`).

CONF-03 · STILT HEIGHT (2.50m CAP) & MANDATORY 6.6 ECS PARKING AMENDED IN 2026 PLAN

Problem: Under **Byelaw 2(136), 29(iii) & 32(3)**, stilt parking is excluded from FAR and building height *only up to `2.50m` height*, and **Table 21** mandates `1.0 ECS/unit + 10% visitor parking`.

Impact: The legacy `3.20m (10'6")` ground floor with a rear 1BHK lost the stilt height exemption and provided only 4 car bays for 7 units.

Implemented Amendment: Set Stilt Floor height to **`2.50m (8'2.5")`** (`≥2.40m` clear beam headway) and dedicated the full ground floor to **6 Car Bays + 10 Two-Wheelers (`6.6+ ECS`)** for the 6 upper-floor 2BHK units.

CONF-04 · FIRE NOC AT ≥9.0m HEIGHT VS. LOW-RISK G+2 SELF-CERTIFICATION DUAL APPROVAL PATHWAY

Problem: **Byelaw 6(6)(c)** lowers the Fire Authority NOC trigger to **`9.0m` height** (down from `15.0m` in 2014), while **Byelaw 9 Table 2** defines buildings up to **`10.0m` (G+2) on plots `≤300 m²`** as **Low Risk** (eligible for immediate ETPA self-certification).

Impact: Building 3 upper floors (`Stilt + 3`, height `9.60m` excl. stilt / `12.10m` total) enters **Moderate Risk** and triggers Fire NOC + BSDMA earthquake certification.

Implemented Amendment: Use the top-bar switcher to select either **Stilt+3 Moderate-Risk (6 Units, Fire NOC + ETPA)** or **Stilt+2 Low-Risk (`8.90m` height, 4 Units, Instant ETPA Self-Certification)**.

1. Full-Width 5'0" (1.52m) South Rear Setback

By stepping the rear building line back `5'0" (1.52m ≥ 1.50m)` across the full `33'0"` width per **BBL 2026 Table 13**, both South bedrooms and the South Living Hall now open directly onto a legal rear open space—eliminating any zero-boundary rear variance.

2. Exterior Window Access for All Bedrooms (Cl. 23(7))

Under **Byelaw 23(7)**, habitable rooms not abutting front, rear, or side open spaces require a `3.0m × 3.0m` interior courtyard. Our amended plan places Bedroom 2 along the East/North/South open perimeter or connects it to the exterior setbacks so the `3'6" × 8'2"` West shaft serves strictly as a **Byelaw 23(17) Service & Bathroom Duct**.

3. 18.3m (60'0") Septic-to-Borewell Separation (Cl. 23(10))

**Byelaw 23(10)** mandates `≥ 18.0 m (59'1")` between any drinking water well and sewage dispersion. Placing the `8,000L` watertight RCC Septic Tank at the NW corner (`X=2', Y=2'`) and the `6"` Deep Borewell at the SE setback (`X=31'6", Y=59'`) yields a **`60'0" (18.3 m)` diagonal separation**.

4. Mandatory Rainwater Harvesting (Byelaw 24)

**Byelaw 24** requires a complete Rainwater Harvesting (RWH) proposal on every plot `≥ 100 sq.m`. Our `202.34 sq.m` plot provides a dedicated `4'0" × 4'0" × 6'0"` dual-media RWH recharge pit in the `3'4" (1.02m)` East setback fed by 4 rooftop rainwater downpipes.
